# Cricket Conscience Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-05

Cricket Conscience is a skills-only ChatGPT/Codex plugin. It does not operate an external server and does not independently collect, transmit, sell, or store personal data.

## Data processed

When Cricket is invoked, its instructions may be applied by ChatGPT or Codex to the conversation content and context already available to the host product. Cricket does not receive a separate copy of that content through a developer-operated backend.

## Purpose

The plugin instructions are used only to review response fidelity, evidence and certainty, authority boundaries, and behavioral overreach.

## Recipients

Cricket has no developer-operated external service and therefore does not independently disclose conversation data to external recipients. Use of ChatGPT and Codex remains subject to OpenAI's own product terms and privacy practices.

## Retention

Cricket itself has no external data store and therefore has no independent retention period. Conversation, account, and plugin-related retention by the host product is controlled by the applicable OpenAI product settings and policies.

## User controls

Users control whether they install or invoke the plugin and may uninstall or disable it through ChatGPT plugin controls. Cricket does not grant itself access to external accounts or tools.

## Other tools and plugins

If a user separately invokes another connector, plugin, or tool while Cricket is active, that system's own privacy terms and permissions govern its data handling. Cricket does not become the operator of those services.

## Changes and contact

Material changes to this policy will be published in this repository. Questions or issues may be filed at https://github.com/thebrazenbeard/cricket/issues.
